Block specific contacts from creating helpdesk tickets by sending an
email, replying instead with an automatic notification.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

Go to a contact's form and, under the *Helpdesk* tab, check *Block
ticket creation by email*.

The notification sent to blocked contacts can be customized from
*Settings > Technical > Email > Templates*, editing the *Helpdesk
Ticket: Creation Blocked* template.

Usage
=====

When a contact with *Block ticket creation by email* enabled sends an
email to a helpdesk alias, no ticket is created. Instead, an automatic
email is sent back to them explaining that they are not authorized to
create tickets by email.

Ticket creation from the backend or the customer portal is not affected
by this restriction.

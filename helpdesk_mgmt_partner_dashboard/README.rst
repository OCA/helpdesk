Replace the Helpdesk team dashboard with one grouped by customer.

This module can be useful for organizations where dividing by helpdesk
team is less informative when looking at the list of pending tasks than
being able to see which contact they are assigned to.

The dashboard only lists contacts with no parent company, and only when
they or any of their child contacts have open tickets assigned to them;
each contact's counters include its own tickets plus its children's.
